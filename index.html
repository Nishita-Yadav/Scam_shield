Scam warning signs

Upfront fees

Registration, training or kit charges before you start work.

Unrealistic salary

Very high pay for simple work or no experience.

Urgency

"Apply today" or "limited seats" pressure to skip checks.

Suspicious URLs

Shortened links and brand-new domains that copy a real company.

Fake recruiter identity

Free email addresses, chat-only contact, no verifiable profile.

Sensitive information requests

Aadhaar, PAN, bank details or OTPs asked for before an offer.

Safety tips

  • Never pay to get a job, training or equipment.
  • Find the role on the company's own careers page.
  • Check that recruiter emails come from the company's domain.
  • Keep ID documents and bank details until you have a verified offer letter.
  • Search the company name plus "scam" and read recent reports.
